## Weapon Features (Passive)
1. Hitting **4 times** triggers **Shrapnel**.
2. Crit hits count as **2 hits**.
3. Crit hits grant Shrapnel trigger chance **+1** for 1 s.
4. Shrapnel Crit DMG **+30 %**.

**Shrapnel**: Upon hitting an enemy, deal Weapon DMG (≈50–60 % Attack) to a different random unhit part. Can Crit, hit Weakspots, decays with distance.
