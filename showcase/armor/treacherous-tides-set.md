# Treacherous Tides Set

**LEGENDARY** · 4 pieces · HP-scaling

## Lore
Treacherous Tides rewards those who fight on the edge. The lower your health and sanity, the deadlier you become.

## World / Set Bonuses (exact – oncehumandb 2026)

| Pieces | Bonus |
|--------|-------|
| **1**  | Weapon DMG Reduction **+10 %** |
| **2**  | When HP **< 70 %** → Weapon + Status DMG **+12 %** |
| **3**  | HP reduction from low Sanity decreased by **40 %**. Weapon + Status DMG **+10 %** and further increases as Sanity drops (max **+28 %** at 30 % Sanity). |
| **4**  | When HP **< 40 %** generates a shield equal to **20 % of Max HP**. Cooldown **8 s**. |

## Notes
Reworked in 3.0.6 – DMG scaling now primarily HP-based instead of pure Sanity.
